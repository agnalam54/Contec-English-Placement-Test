Section 1: Reading Comprehension (5 Points)
A New Way of Working

Many companies are changing the way their employees work. In the past, most employees worked in an office from Monday to Friday. Today, however, many companies allow employees to work remotely for part or all of the week.

Working remotely has several advantages. Employees can save time because they do not have to travel to work every day. Some people also find it easier to concentrate when they work from home. Companies can benefit too because they may need less office space.

However, remote work also has disadvantages. Employees may sometimes feel isolated, and communication can be more difficult when people are not working in the same place. For this reason, many companies use a combination of office and remote work.

This system is often called hybrid working. It allows employees to work from home on some days and from the office on others. Many businesses believe that this approach offers a good balance between flexibility and teamwork.

1. What change have many companies made?

2. What is an advantage for employees?

3. How can companies benefit?

4. What is a stated disadvantage of remote work?

5. What is "hybrid working"?
